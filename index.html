Mandatory Backfill List

Employees pick their forced-hire dates at the start of the fiscal year (Oct 1). Download a sheet with every date, the platoon on duty, and the platoon eligible to be forced already filled in — add names and upload it back. Re-uploading a corrected sheet updates rather than duplicating.

Only one platoon can be forced on any given date. Nobody may work 72 hours straight, so the eligible platoon is the one working neither the day before nor the day after. On a C-shift day 1 that is A shift; on C-shift day 2 it is B shift. The import rejects anyone from the wrong platoon.
